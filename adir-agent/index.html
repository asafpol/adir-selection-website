ADIR-Agent

A private assistant for ADIR Selection business filing.

About the application

ADIR-Agent reads emails and files attachments, such as invoices and packing lists, in the ADIR Selection business filing system.

The application accesses only the owner's Gmail account, asafpol@gmail.com, through Google account authorization. Its developer and owner is its sole user. It is a personal tool for the owner's business work and is not offered to other users.

This page provides public information about the application.

Contact and support
