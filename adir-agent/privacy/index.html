ADIR-Agent Privacy Policy

Published: 20 October 2026

Application and owner

ADIR-Agent is a private assistant used by its developer and owner for ADIR Selection business work. Its sole user is asafpol@gmail.com. It is not offered to other users.

Google data accessed and used

With the owner's Google account authorization, ADIR-Agent reads emails and their attachments from the owner's Gmail account. It uses this access to identify and file business attachments, such as invoices and packing lists, in the business filing system.

Storage and retention

Filed attachments are saved in the business filing system locally on the owner's own device. They are retained until the owner manually deletes them.

A Google authorization token is securely saved to keep the application connected to the owner's account. Apart from the filed attachments and this authorization token, the application does not save Gmail data.

Third-party processing and disclosure

The application connects to Google to access Gmail. It does not send the Gmail data it accesses to hosting services, AI providers, or other third-party providers. The filed attachments are stored locally on the owner's device.

Deletion and revoking access

The owner deletes filed attachments manually from the local business filing system.

To revoke ADIR-Agent's access to Gmail, the owner can open Google Account third-party connections, select ADIR-Agent, and remove its access. Revoking access prevents further authorized Gmail access; it does not delete attachments already saved in the local filing system.

Contact

For questions about this policy or ADIR-Agent, contact the developer and owner at asafpol@gmail.com.
